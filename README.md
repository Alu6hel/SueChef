SueChef
Overview
SueChef is a privacy-first legal preparation platform designed to bridge the gap between complex civil procedure and everyday accessibility. For anyone who has never engaged with the legal system, the process can feel like an intimidating maze of foreign terminology, strict deadlines, and hidden traps. SueChef demystifies the entire workflow, turning raw disputes into structured, organized cases while prioritizing absolute privacy and legal best practices.

(Disclaimer: SueChef is a legal preparation and organizational tool, not a licensed attorney. It provides structured guidance and document drafting support, but does not constitute formal legal advice.)

Features Built for Beginners & Everyday Users
Stepping into the legal world for the first time requires safety rails and clear guidance. SueChef includes specialized features designed to protect first-time litigants from common procedural mistakes:

Legalese-to-English Decoder: No law degree required. The app translates dense procedural jargon, Latin terms, and formal court requirements into clear, everyday language so you always understand what a step actually means.

The Statute of Limitations Watcher: The golden rule of law is that time kills claims. The app prompts you to input key dates to flag your jurisdiction's filing deadlines, ensuring you never miss your legal window.

Evidence Preservation Best Practices: Beginners often ruin digital evidence by screenshotting improperly or altering metadata. SueChef provides step-by-step guides on how to properly log texts, emails, and photos to ensure they remain admissible in court.

The Attorney Hand-Off Packet: A massive financial best practice. If you decide halfway through that you want a real lawyer, SueChef packages your timeline, evidence, and drafts into a clean, professional dossier. Handing this to an attorney saves you thousands of dollars in billable hours.

Core Operational Features
The Claim Kitchen (Evaluator): A guided, step-by-step questionnaire that helps you organize the facts, damages, and timeline of your dispute to evaluate whether your claim has structural merit before paying court filing fees.

Complaint Builder: Automatically structures your raw narrative into formal legal complaint templates compliant with standard civil pleading requirements.

Process & Service Tracker: Serving notice to a defendant can be legally tricky. This feature tracks formal delivery deadlines, manages process server logs, and stores proof-of-service documentation in one secure dashboard.

Ironclad Confidentiality: Legal disputes are deeply private. SueChef operates entirely offline with zero cloud telemetry, ensuring your opposing party or outside entities never gain access to your private legal strategy.

Technical Architecture
Legal preparation involves high-stakes data that cannot afford to leak or experience latency.

Bare-Metal Performance: The core document generation and validation logic are built in Rust, ensuring memory safety, rapid file formatting, and bulletproof reliability.

Local Secure Storage: All case notes, drafts, and evidence logs are housed in an encrypted, local SQLite database on your hardware, preventing third-party data harvesting.
